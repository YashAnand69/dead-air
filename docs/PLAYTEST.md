# Five-minute setup for a first playtest

Give your friend only the game link, not the solution or source documents.

“Try this short radio mystery as you normally would. Tell me what you expect when you use a control. I won't explain the puzzle unless you want to stop.”

Observe time to first clue, confusing controls, questions tried, hints requested, and whether they reach an ending. Do not coach. Ask afterwards: What happened? Which two records persuaded you? Did the AI suggestions help? Would you play another case?

Ask permission before recording or publishing quotations. One person is a usability trial, not proof of broad appeal. No real-user trial has yet been recorded for this release.
